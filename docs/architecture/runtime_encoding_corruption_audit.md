### Retained because cleanup would change parsing behavior

`core/tasks/scheduler.py` still contains corrupted literals in behavior-bearing
keyword lists and regular expressions. They occur in these areas (line numbers
are from the post-cleanup file):

- Lines 6850 and 6852: verification markers.
- Lines 7455-7456 and 7463: document action/summary keywords.
- Lines 7577-7582: hello-world intent candidates.
- Lines 7619-7624: write-content extraction regular expressions.

Changing or deleting these values would alter user-input recognition and is
therefore outside this encoding-only cleanup. They require a separate parser
contract change with focused tests.

No requested corruption signature was found in `core/adaptive/**`,
`core/agent/code_chain_controlled_self_edit_bridge.py`, or `tests/**`.
Legible Chinese text was treated as valid content, not corruption.
